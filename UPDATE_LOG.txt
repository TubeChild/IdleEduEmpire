  IDLE EDU EMPIRE  —  FULL CONTENT OVERVIEW
  Everything that is in the game right now

This log covers every feature, building, upgrade, and
system currently in the game. Great for testing!

BUILDINGS  (13 total)
─────────────────────
You earn Knowledge Points (KP) per second from buildings,
and more from clicking. Buildings unlock as your total
all-time KP grows — not all are available from the start.

  1.  Classroom
  2.  Library
  3.  Science Lab
  4.  Computer Lab
  5.  Sports Hall
  6.  Art Studio
  7.  University Wing
  8.  Research Centre
  9.  Innovation Hub
  10. Space Academy
  11. World Campus
  12. Quantum Institute
  13. Nexus of Knowledge

  Each building has a star milestone system.
  At certain ownership counts a building levels up
  (⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐), granting a permanent output bonus.
  The milestone counts vary per building.


UPGRADES  (34 total)
─────────────────────
Bought with KP. Each doubles the output of its target
building. Some upgrade click power instead.

  Building upgrades:
    Classroom ×3, Library ×3, Science Lab ×2,
    Computer Lab ×2, Sports Hall ×2, Art Studio ×2,
    University Wing ×2, Research Centre ×2,
    Innovation Hub ×3, Space Academy ×3,
    World Campus ×2, Quantum Institute ×2,
    Nexus of Knowledge ×2

  Click upgrades ×4:
    Sharp Pencil → Fountain Pen → Laptop → Gaming Tablet


BUILDING SYNERGIES
──────────────────
Some buildings passively boost each other based on how
many of the linked building you own. There are 12
synergy links across the full building chain — for
example, Libraries help Classrooms, and Research Centres
boost University Wings and Innovation Hubs.


CLICKING & COMBO
─────────────────
Click the main school building to earn KP manually.
Build up a combo by clicking quickly — each hit within
a short window adds to the streak and multiplies your
click power. The combo resets if you stop clicking.
Base max combo is 10 (can be raised by skills).


FOCUS POINTS
─────────────
Focus Points (FP) regenerate over time up to a cap.
Spend them on special active abilities:

  ⚡ Study Burst     — earn a burst of KP instantly
  ⚡ Overdrive       — temporary KPS multiplier
  ⚡ Recall          — earn 10 minutes of offline KP now
  ⚡ Inspiration     — temporary click power boost

The FP bar and ability buttons are in the left sidebar.
Skills can raise the cap and the regen rate.


EVENTS  (20 types)
────────────────────
Random events pop up during play. Click to collect them.

  Common events:
    School Fundraiser, Pop Quiz Craze, Science Fair Day,
    Time Capsule Opened, Star Student

  Uncommon events:
    Inspiring Guest Speaker, Education Grant (+5 Merit),
    Inspection Outstanding, Coding Hackathon,
    Scholarship Wave (+7 Merit), Research Published,
    Scholar in Residence, Research Breakthrough

  Rare events:
    Faculty Hire (permanent building bonus!),
    School Goes Viral, Alumni Donation Drive,
    Mystery Benefactor, Academic Olympiad (click ×20),
    National Medal (+12 Merit), World Education Record

  Inspector Events (separate system):
    The school inspector arrives and gives you a task.
    Complete it in time for a bonus, or face a small
    debuff if you fail.


FACULTY HIRES
─────────────
Faculty Hire events (rare) permanently boost a specific
building's output. The building is chosen based on what
you currently own. Collecting 1, 5, and 10 faculty
hires earns achievements.


DAILY MISSIONS
──────────────
Three missions reset every day:
  • Click X times
  • Earn X KP
  • Buy X buildings

Complete all three to earn Merit Points and progress
toward daily streak achievements (1 / 7 / 30 days).


SEASONS
────────
The game calendar has four seasons, each with a bonus:
  Spring  — +8% KP/s
  Summer  — +12% click power
  Autumn  — +15% diploma gain on prestige
  Winter  — +25% offline earnings efficiency

Experiencing all four seasons earns an achievement.


OFFLINE EARNINGS
────────────────
KP accumulates while you are away, up to a cap
(default 8 hours, extendable via skills and upgrades).
An offline earnings popup tells you what you earned
when you return. Winter gives a bonus to offline rates.


CAMPUS VIEW
────────────
The left sidebar shows a live mini-view of your campus.
Buildings appear visually as you buy them.
The season badge and current KP/s are displayed here too.


SCHOOL NAME
────────────
You can rename your school in the Settings tab.
Your school name appears in the news ticker and
dynamic news headlines.


NEWS TICKER
────────────
A scrolling ticker at the bottom shows school news.
Dynamic headlines are generated when you:
  • Open a building for the first time
  • Hit a KP milestone (100 → 1 Trillion)
  • Unlock an achievement
  • Prestige (graduation ceremony headlines)

  PRESTIGE SYSTEM  (4 layers)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Prestige resets your KP and buildings but grants
permanent bonuses. There are four prestige layers,
each with its own shop that persists forever.

LAYER 1 — DIPLOMAS  (Prestige tab)
────────────────────────────────────
Prestige when you reach the KP threshold (shown on
the Prestige tab). You receive Diplomas based on your
total KP. More Diplomas = better permanent bonuses.

  Diploma Shop  (11 upgrades — survive all resets):
    Endowed Chair, Alumni Network, Research Grant,
    Campus Expansion (−15% costs), Click Mastery,
    Distinguished Faculty, Ivy League Status (−25% costs),
    Nobel Legacy, Golden Campus, Grand Endowment,
    Extended Leave (+12 hrs offline cap)


LAYER 2 — HONORS  (Legacy tab)
────────────────────────────────
Convert 15 Diplomas into 1 Honor.

  Honor Shop  (10 upgrades):
    Honorary Lecture, Research Fellowship, Merit Scholarship,
    Extended Study Hall, Honours Desk, Honours Discount,
    Academic Prestige, Synergy Amplifier,
    Diploma Surplus, Apex Scholar

  Also: buy Historical Scholars with Honors (see below).


LAYER 3 — ENDOWMENTS  (Legacy tab)
─────────────────────────────────────
Convert 5 Honors into 1 Endowment.

  Endowment Shop  (6 upgrades):
    Foundation Grant, Endowed Clicks, Endowed Campus,
    Distinguished Faculty II, Endowment Study Cap,
    Eternal Legacy


LAYER 4 — ALUMNI POINTS  (Legacy tab)
───────────────────────────────────────
Convert Endowments into Alumni Points (rates improve
with skills). Alumni Points never reset.

  Alumni Shop  (7 upgrades):
    Pioneer Legacy, Tenure Track, Research Network,
    Alumni Discount, Century Endowment,
    Distinguished Alumni, Infinite Knowledge

  Research Legacy (repeatable):
    Spend 2 Alumni Points to permanently gain +10% KPS.
    Can be bought as many times as you like.

  WORLDS — 8 EXTRA ZONES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Worlds tab lets you manage 8 extra zones.
Each runs independently and has its own buildings,
upgrades, prestige layers, and mechanic.
All zones earn KP in the background even when you
are looking at a different tab.

WHAT EVERY ZONE HAS
────────────────────
  • 10 themed buildings
  • 12 upgrades
  • 4 prestige layers with their own currency names
  • 1 passive mechanic (runs automatically)
  • 1 active ability (triggered manually, usually costs
    a prestige currency or the mechanic meter)
  • A lock requirement — see unlock tree below


THE 8 ZONES
────────────

  Zone 2  The Ruins  🏚
    Restore an abandoned school to its former glory.
    Passive: Decay — buildings deteriorate over time,
    reducing output by up to −50%.
    Active: Restoration Rush — spend 1 Restoration Cert
    to wipe the decay instantly.
    Instructor: khaki explorer outfit, orange hard hat.

  Zone 3  The Future  🚀
    Run a hyper-advanced school on a space station.
    Passive: Automation — slowly builds a KPS bonus.
    Active: Overclock — spend 1 Sync Token for an
    instant KP burst.
    Instructor: silver jumpsuit, sci-fi dome helmet.

  Zone 4  Ancient Academy  🏛
    Build the great schools of antiquity.
    Passive: Discourse — fills over time for a KPS bonus.
    Active: Great Debate — spend 1 Discourse Token
    for a large instant KP burst.
    Instructor: white toga, green laurel wreath, scroll.

  Zone 5  Moon Colony  🌙
    The first school on the Moon — low gravity included.
    Passive: Orbit Bonus — scales with total buildings
    owned, adding a steady KPS multiplier.
    Active: EVA Launch — spend 1 Moon Rock to earn
    5 minutes of KPS instantly.
    Instructor: white spacesuit, bubble helmet.
    Unlocks: Zone 3 prestige ×1

  Zone 6  Magical Realm  ✨
    A school of sorcery where mana powers learning.
    Passive: Mana Pool — fills over time, +50% KPS
    when full.
    Active: Cast Spell — drain all mana for an
    instant KP burst.
    Instructor: purple robes, tall pointy wizard hat.
    Unlocks: Zone 3 Alumni ×1

  Zone 7  Prehistoric  🦕
    Spark the very first flame of learning.
    Passive: Discovery — fills slowly.
    Active: Great Hunt — triggers a large KP burst
    and resets the meter.
    Instructor: brown fur outfit, bone hair-pin.
    Unlocks: Zone 2 Alumni ×1

  Zone 8  The Heavens  ☁
    Teach among the gods.
    Passive: Divine Grace — fills over time for
    a steady KPS bonus.
    Active: Prayer — spend Divine Grace for an
    instant KP burst.
    Instructor: glowing white robes, golden halo, wing nubs.
    Unlocks: Zone 4 prestige ×1

  Zone 9  The Underworld  🔥
    Knowledge at a price. Sin builds up and punishes you.
    Passive: Sin Meter — accumulates and reduces KPS
    the higher it gets.
    Active: Dark Pact — clear your sin, but waiting too
    long triggers a punishment timer.
    Instructor: dark red robes, small horns, glowing red eyes.
    Unlocks: Zone 4 Alumni ×1


ZONE UNLOCK TREE
─────────────────
Zones open up as you progress in earlier ones.

  Modern School  →  The Ruins        (prestige Zone 1 once)
  Modern School  →  The Future       (earn 1 Alumni Point)

  The Ruins      →  Ancient Academy  (prestige Zone 2 once)
  The Ruins      →  Prehistoric      (earn Zone 2 Alumni ×1)

  The Future     →  Moon Colony      (prestige Zone 3 once)

  Ancient Academy →  The Underworld  (earn Zone 4 Alumni ×1)

The lock screen in the Worlds tab always tells you
exactly what is needed before a zone is available.


COSMIC WISDOM
─────────────
Prestiges and currency conversions in any zone earn
Cosmic Wisdom (CW) — a special cross-zone currency.
Shown at the bottom of the zone list.

  Zone prestige   →  1 CW
  Prestige → L2   →  5 CW
  L2 → L3         →  15 CW
  L3 → L4         →  50 CW

A Multiverse Shop to spend CW is coming in a future update.

DIPLOMA FORMULA TUNED
──────────────────────
Diplomas earned on prestige used to grow too fast (square
root). It now uses a cube root so the pace feels more
rewarding over a longer game, not front-loaded.
  Old: ~232 diplomas at second prestige with heavy play
  New: ~47 diplomas — much more meaningful progression.

CRASH FIX — OVERFLOW ON PRESTIGE
──────────────────────────────────
Prestiging with extreme building counts caused an
OverflowError from the diploma multiplier (1.02 ^ diplomas).
Fixed by switching to a log-based formula:
  diploma_mult = 1.0 + ln(1 + diplomas) × 1.5
This grows meaningfully but never overflows.
A crash.log file is now written if the game crashes.

SPRITE PTERODACTYLS FOR ZONE 7
────────────────────────────────
Zone 7 (Prehistoric) pterodactyls now use the pterosaur1/
pterosaur2 PNG sprites instead of procedural line art.
Key details:

  • Sprites are recoloured at load time using luminance
    remapping (numpy): the teal/blue pixel-art is converted
    to warm brown/tan (e.g. teal (54,197,244) → tan (188,133,70))
    to match the zone's earthy palette.
  • Wing animation is driven by math.sin(self.t) — the same
    phase variable that governs the flap rate — so each
    pterodactyl flaps at its own natural frequency.
  • Left and right-facing versions are pre-baked at load time
    to avoid per-frame surface flips.
  • Falls back to the procedural Pterodactyl if the PNG
    assets cannot be loaded.

HERO PASSIVE BONUSES (game.py)
────────────────────────────────
The created hero now grants six permanent passive bonuses
while it exists, derived from stat scores (0–50 each) set
at creation time:

  Intelligence  → +0.5% global KPS per point  (max +25%)
  Agility       → +0.5% click power per point  (max +25%)
  Resilience    → -0.2% building costs per point (max -10%)
  Tech Power    → +0.5% all-building KPS per point (max +25%)
  Reputation    → +0.5 extra Diplomas per prestige per point
                  (max +25 Diplomas/prestige)

  _global_mult()        — Intelligence + Transcendence

  _building_mult()      — Tech Power (additive, all buildings)
  diplomas_on_prestige  — Reputation (additive to base)

  XP/s = Zone 1 KPS × 0.0002

  Level 1→2:   200 XP
  Level 2→3:   340 XP
  Level 5→6:  1,680 XP
  Level 10→11: 20,600 XP
  Level 20→21:  ~3M XP

Every level-up awards 1 Stat Point. Every 5 levels a news
ticker fires:  "Hero of X reached Level Y! (N points to spend)"

New methods:
  _hero_xp_needed(level) — static formula: 200 × 1.7^(level-1)
  update_hero(dt)        — called from update(), accumulates XP,
                           handles multi-level-ups per tick
  train_hero_stat(key)   — spends 1 stat point on the given stat,
                           no upper cap (bonuses scale linearly)

Hero dict gains three new fields (save-compatible via defaults):
  xp           (float)
  level        (int, starts at 1)
  stat_points  (int)
